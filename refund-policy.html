Your statutory right, and what happens to it

Consumers in the EU and UK normally have 14 days to withdraw from an online purchase. For digital content downloaded immediately, that right ends once the download begins — and at checkout you agree to immediate access, which is what lets you read the guide on the spot instead of waiting a fortnight. That agreement is what this policy rests on.

Paddle is the merchant of record, so their buyer terms also apply to the transaction, and nothing here removes a right you have under the law where you live.

How a refund reaches you

When one is due, Paddle returns the money to your original payment method. Banks typically take 3–10 days to show it, which is out of my hands and theirs.
