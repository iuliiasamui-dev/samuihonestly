How is this different from the free guide?

The free guide is a list — 300+ places, no opinions attached. This one takes a position: it tells you which area suits you, what will annoy you about it, and who should stay away. The list is the easy part; the judgement is what took three years.

What format is it?

A 17-page PDF. Opens on any device, works offline, yours forever.

Does it cover hotels?

It names the places I'd actually point people to in each area, across every budget — but it deliberately doesn't rank them. The area decides your holiday; the hotel is a detail.

Is it useful if I'm coming in summer?

Especially then. The east and west coasts swap roles around May, and most guides are written as if December is the only season. Each area entry says what it's like on both sides of that switch.

Yes. When something material changes — a beach erodes, an area gets a new road, a place I recommended stops being worth it — I update the file and re-send it free.
